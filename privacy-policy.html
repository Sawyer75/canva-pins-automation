Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Canva Pins Automation is a personal application used by the developer to manage and publish content to their own Pinterest account.

Information We Access

The application may access Pinterest account information, Pins, boards, and other data necessary to publish and manage the developer's own Pinterest content through the Pinterest API.

How Information Is Used

Information accessed through the Pinterest API is used only to operate the application and to create, organize, publish, and manage the developer's own Pinterest content.

Data Sharing

The application does not sell, rent, or share Pinterest user data with third parties for advertising or other unrelated purposes.

Data Retention

Pinterest data is retained only as necessary for the operation of the application and the management of the developer's own Pinterest account.

Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services required for hosting, automation, or content management. Such services are used only when necessary to operate the application.

Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the GitHub profile associated with this project.
